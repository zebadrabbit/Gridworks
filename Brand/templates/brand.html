The whole system, on one page. The mark and the wordmark are generated by src/logo.py; the colours are lifted from the game's own src/style.css into src/tokens.css; the contrast ratios below are recomputed in this page every time it loads, so a colour that gets nudged and quietly stops meeting AA turns red here instead of staying green in a comment.

The mark

A G built the way the game builds a factory: a run of wire between named nodes, with a pad at every corner and a hub where the run terminates. The letterform is not decorated with a circuit motif — the wiring is the letter. That is why it reduces: a run of wire with a terminal on it is still a G at 16 px, where an illustration of a factory would be a smudge.

Reduction

The full mark carries three detail layers: the interior lattice, the swollen corner pads, and the lit cores on top of them. Below roughly 24 px all three stop resolving and start reading as noise, so anything smaller uses the simple mark — one colour, silhouette only. The favicon already does. Minimum size for the full mark is 20 px.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space of at least 0.30× the mark's height on every side — nothing, including type, another logo or the edge of the canvas, comes inside it. The mark already carries an aperture and a protruding hub; crowding it closes both.

Colour

Cyan is the brand, and on the void it is not merely a graphic colour — means it is a perfectly good colour for body text, which is the opposite of the usual neon problem. Every ratio below is measured live.

On paper

The trap runs the other way. Put the same cyan on a white README, a slide or a store page and it measures — Use --g-cyan-ink whenever cyan has to sit on a light ground, and the -ink variants of the three status colours with it.

Type

The wordmark ships as outlines drawn on the grid, so nothing here has to match it. Running text is the same system stack the game uses: a browser game that blocks first paint on a webfont fetch would be missing its own point, and this pack inherits that constraint on purpose. The scale is a 1.25 major third off 13 px — the size the HUD actually runs at, rather than a number chosen to look tidy in a spec.

The grid

One module, taken from the source: TILE in src/sim.js. Every spacing value in tokens.css is a division of it, so a brand layout and a factory layout are on the same grid.
